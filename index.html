Delete Your Anceega Account

Anceega allows users to request deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Anceega account using either of the following methods:

1. From the Anceega App

Open the Anceega app, go to Settings, select Delete Account, and follow the instructions to confirm the deletion of your account.

2. By Email

You can also request account deletion by sending an email to info@anceega.com with the subject Account Deletion Request.

Please include the email address associated with your Anceega account in your request so that we can identify your account.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is completed, the following account-associated data will be deleted where applicable:

  • Account and profile information
  • Email address associated with the account
  • Phone number associated with the account
  • Professional information
  • Skills and experience information
  • User-generated posts and associated content
  • Other data directly associated with your account

Data Retention

Some information may be retained when required by law, for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security purposes, or resolving disputes. Any retained information will be deleted when the applicable retention period ends.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after receiving the request.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding account deletion or your data, please contact us at:

info@anceega.com

Important: Account deletion permanently removes your account and associated data that is eligible for deletion. This action cannot be undone.
