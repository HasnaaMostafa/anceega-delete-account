Delete Your Anceega Account

If you would like to delete your Anceega account and associated data, you can submit a deletion request by contacting us at:

info@anceega.com

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to info@anceega.com.
  2. Use "Account Deletion Request" as the email subject.
  3. Include the email address associated with your Anceega account.
  4. Our team will review and process your request.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is completed, the following account-associated data will be deleted where applicable:

  • Account and profile information
  • Email address and phone number associated with the account
  • Profile data, skills, and professional information
  • User-generated posts and associated account content
  • Other data directly associated with your account

Data Retention

Some information may be retained when required by law, for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security purposes, or resolving disputes. Any retained information will be deleted when the applicable retention period ends.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after receiving the request.

If you have any questions regarding account deletion or your data, please contact us at info@anceega.com.
